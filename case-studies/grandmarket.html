GrandMarket Analytics Platform

Forecasting, anomaly detection & executive analytics · Python, synthetic data

github.com/tolstii/grandmarket-analytics

An end-to-end analytics platform for multi-store wholesale and retail operations, built to show enterprise-grade capability (the kind of thing SAP IBP or Blue Yonder sell) at mid-market scale and cost. Demand forecasting, statistical anomaly detection, scenario P&L, and a DAMA data-quality scorecard, all in one Streamlit app over synthetic data, and all in plain Python with the math visible.

The problem

A mid-market operator wants what the enterprise suites promise, without the enterprise price or a data-science team: a forecast it can trust, early warning when something drifts, a P&L it can stress, and confidence that the numbers underneath are clean. The design constraint was deliberate: no black-box ML library, so every number on the dashboard can be explained and audited.

Approach

synthetic ops data→ DQ scorecard→ forecasting→ anomaly + alerts→ scenario P&L→ executive view

Hand-built forecasting

Three methods, written from scratch over pandas so the mechanics stay visible: a moving-average baseline, exponential smoothing for recent-trend weight, and a Holt-Winters model with level, trend, and weekly seasonality (alpha/beta/gamma, hand-coded) plus holiday multipliers. No statsmodels, no SARIMAX. The point was to understand and own the forecast, not import it.

Statistical anomaly detection

Anomalies are flagged with rolling mean and standard deviation, with confidence bands (a 90% interval at 1.645 sigma) rather than a black-box detector. Supplier reliability is scored from delivery-delay variance. Simple, explainable, and tuned to the data, which is exactly what a mid-market team can maintain without a data scientist.

Scenario P&L and executive view

A four-scenario P&L model (base, upside, downside, stress) quantifies revenue-at-risk, and the executive layer rolls everything a CEO and CFO need into one screen. The analytics layers (core, forecasting, alerts, executive) are separated so each is testable on its own.

Data quality underneath it all

A DAMA-aligned scorecard monitors the operational tables across five quality dimensions with rule-based alerting, so a forecast is never built on data that silently drifted. Quality is the foundation, not an afterthought.

What the platform reports

97.8% overall data-quality score 5-dimension scorecard, 10-rule alerting 4-scenario P&L with revenue-at-risk Holt-Winters + exponential smoothing One-screen CEO / CFO dashboard
Built on synthetic data as a capability demonstration. The deliberate choice throughout is plain Python over a framework: every forecast and every anomaly flag can be traced to arithmetic you can read, which is the right trade-off for a team that has to own and defend the numbers.
